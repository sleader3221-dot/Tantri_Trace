## Idea Title / आइडिया का शीर्षक

**TantriTrace: QR-Verified Handloom Authenticity & Weaver Story Platform**

## Idea in Brief with Problem Statement / संक्षेप में आइडिया और समस्या का विवरण

Handloom buyers, retailers, and export partners often cannot verify whether a textile is genuinely handmade, GI-linked, or connected to the named artisan. This weakens buyer trust, allows counterfeit products to capture premium prices, and keeps weavers invisible after their products enter the market. As a result, the cultural value, labor time, and maker identity behind handloom products are often lost.

TantriTrace solves this by giving every handloom product a QR-style digital trace, SHA-256 authenticity certificate, named weaver profile, product journey, and tamper-evident ledger record. A buyer or retailer can scan or enter a product ID, certificate ID, or trace hash to verify the item instantly. The consumer view shows the weaver story, craft context, origin, GI or certification mark, impact receipt, and shareable certificate. The retailer view supports batch scanning, fraud reporting, and risk scoring for suspicious duplicate or unknown tags. Cooperatives and government programs can use the API-ready registry, JSON export, and ledger verification to scale the same trust layer across handloom clusters.
